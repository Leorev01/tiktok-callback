GlowPost

GlowPost is a personal tool that schedules and publishes my own slideshow and video posts to my TikTok account.

How it works

  1. I log in once with TikTok and approve GlowPost (Login Kit).
  2. GlowPost prepares each post from images I've made, with the caption and hashtags I've written.
  3. At the time I've scheduled, it publishes the post to my account through TikTok's Content Posting API.

It only posts to the account that authorised it, and access can be revoked any time in TikTok's app permissions.
