Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

This app is a personal tool used by its owner to schedule and publish their own content to their own TikTok account. It is not offered to other users.

What we access

With the account owner's permission, the app uses TikTok's Login Kit and Content Posting API to read basic profile information (display name, avatar) and to publish photo posts the owner has created.

What we store

The app stores TikTok access and refresh tokens in private, access-controlled cloud storage so it can post on a schedule. Images to be posted are stored at a public URL only so TikTok can retrieve them. No other personal data is collected, sold or shared.

Revoking access

The owner can revoke access at any time in TikTok under Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions. Stored tokens are then deleted.
